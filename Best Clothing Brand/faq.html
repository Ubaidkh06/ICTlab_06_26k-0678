Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about orders, shipping, and fabrics.

  1. What fabrics do you use for your traditional wear?

    We use 100% premium cotton, traditional khaddar, and high-grade wash-and-wear fabrics designed specifically for comfort in all seasons.

  2. How long does nationwide delivery take?

    Standard delivery across major cities in Pakistan takes 3 to 5 working days.

  3. What is your return and exchange policy?

    We offer a 7-day hassle-free return and exchange policy, provided the item is unworn and has its original tags attached.
